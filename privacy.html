Privacy and disclaimer

Smart Trailer Buyer is an educational resource for first-time travel trailer shoppers.

Information collected

When you request the free checklist through the linked Google Form, Smart Trailer Buyer may collect the information you submit, such as your name, email address, tow vehicle, buying timeline, camper interest, and biggest concern.

That information is used to deliver the checklist, understand what first-time buyers need help with, and send relevant follow-up resources when you opt in.

Analytics

The site may use privacy-focused website analytics to understand page visits, referrers, and clicks on checklist links. Analytics should not be used to store sensitive personal details.

Educational guidance only

Smart Trailer Buyer does not replace manufacturer specifications, tow vehicle manuals, dealer documentation, certified inspections, legal advice, financial advice, or qualified safety guidance.

Always verify tow ratings, payload, GVWR, cargo capacity, hitch requirements, pricing, financing, warranty terms, and safety requirements with the appropriate qualified sources before buying or towing.

Affiliate disclosure

Smart Trailer Buyer may add affiliate links or referral links in the future. If that happens, the site will identify those links clearly. Recommended gear should still be checked against your trailer, tow vehicle, campsite setup, and manufacturer requirements.

Contact

For now, use the Smart Trailer Buyer social profiles linked on this site for questions about the checklist or resources.
