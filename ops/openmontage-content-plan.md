# OpenMontage Content Plan

OpenMontage can help make Smart Trailer Buyer content more appealing. Use it as the production engine after the funnel can measure checklist clicks.

## Where It Helps

- Better pacing for TikTok, Reels, and Shorts.
- Word-level or phrase-level captions.
- Voiceover plus music and sound design.
- Repeatable visual templates for towing, budget, dealer, and delivery tips.
- Reference-video analysis when you find a Short/Reel style you want to adapt.
- Batch production once a hook proves it gets checklist requests.

## Best Pipelines For Smart Trailer Buyer

1. `animated-explainer`
   - Best for: dry weight, payload, tongue weight, first-year budget.
   - Style: clean motion graphics, checklist captions, simple diagram moments.

2. `hybrid`
   - Best for: combining real trailer/showroom/campsite footage with support graphics.
   - Style: practical buyer education with B-roll and text overlays.

3. `documentary-montage`
   - Best for: emotional brand pieces about the payoff of buying the right trailer.
   - Style: campsites, roads, families, quiet confidence.

4. `screen-demo`
   - Best for: walking through the checklist, budget worksheet, or future calculator.
   - Style: simple tutorial with captions.

## Production Standard

- Start with one sample before generating a batch.
- Keep one hook per video.
- Make the first 2 seconds obvious without audio.
- Use captions large enough for phone viewing.
- Include the Smart Trailer Buyer name or mark subtly.
- Avoid fear-mongering. Use calm buyer protection.
- Do not make final safety claims. Tell buyers what to verify.
- End with one CTA, not three.

## Reference Prompt

Use this when asking OpenMontage to make a Smart Trailer Buyer Short:

Make a 30-second vertical short for Smart Trailer Buyer about {{topic}}.

Audience: first-time travel trailer buyers.

Tone: calm, practical, buyer-protective, not fear-based.

Structure:
1. Hook in the first 2 seconds.
2. Explain the mistake in plain language.
3. Show 3 to 5 checks buyers should make.
4. End with: Comment CHECKLIST and I will send you the free First-Time Travel Trailer Buyer Checklist.

Style: clean motion graphics, large captions, subtle trailer/camping visuals, warm practical authority, no dealership endorsement.

Verification: captions must be readable on mobile, audio must not clip, and the CTA must appear visually and in narration.
