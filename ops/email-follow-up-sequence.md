# Email Follow-Up Sequence

Goal: turn a checklist request into trust, replies, and eventual toolkit or audit interest.

Use a plain sender name, a direct subject line, and one clear next step per email. Keep the tone practical and buyer-first.

## Email 2 - Towing Tip

Timing: 1 day after signup.

Subject: Do not shop by dry weight alone

Body:

Hi {{first_name}},

One of the easiest first-time buyer mistakes is comparing trailers by dry weight.

Dry weight does not include the real load: battery, propane, water, food, tools, clothes, camping gear, passengers, hitch equipment, and everything else that shows up after purchase.

Before a floorplan wins, check:

- Tow rating
- Payload rating from the driver door sticker
- Trailer GVWR
- Cargo capacity
- Estimated loaded tongue weight
- Hitch and brake controller requirements

If those numbers feel close, slow down and verify before buying.

## Email 3 - Budget Tip

Timing: 3 days after signup.

Subject: The payment is not the whole trailer budget

Body:

Hi {{first_name}},

The monthly payment can make a trailer feel affordable before the full first-year cost is visible.

Before deciding what fits, include:

- Dealer and documentation fees
- Taxes and registration
- Insurance
- Hitch or weight distribution setup
- Brake controller, if needed
- Starter gear
- Storage
- Fuel increase
- Campsite fees
- Maintenance and repair reserve

The right trailer should fit the tow vehicle and the first-year budget.

## Email 4 - Floorplan Tip

Timing: 5 days after signup.

Subject: A floorplan should work after the showroom

Body:

Hi {{first_name}},

A good floorplan should work when people are actually camping, not just when the trailer is clean and empty on the lot.

Before buying, picture:

- A rainy afternoon inside
- Bedtime with everyone tired
- Pets, shoes, bags, food, and wet towels
- Bathroom access during travel
- Fridge access with the slide closed
- Storage after the trailer is loaded
- Setup and teardown when you are ready to go home

The best trailer is the one that fits your real camping style.
