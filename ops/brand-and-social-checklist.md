## Brand Name

Use: Smart Trailer Buyer

## Profile Setup

- Use the same logo or mark on all platforms.
- Use the same display name: Smart Trailer Buyer.
- Put the free checklist first in the bio.
- Pin the dry-weight video or best checklist video once the platform supports pinning.
- Do not claim dealership endorsement.
- Do not use dealership leads, private pricing, internal materials, or customer data.
