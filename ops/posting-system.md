# Posting System

Goal: publish enough useful content to learn which topics create checklist requests.

## Weekly Cadence

- 3 short-form posts per week.
- 1 checklist CTA per post.
- 1 end-of-week review of comments, clicks, form requests, and replies.

## Ten Reusable Short-Form Templates

1. Mistake opener
   - Hook: Do not buy your first travel trailer based on {{bad_assumption}}.
   - Body: Explain what buyers miss.
   - CTA: Comment CHECKLIST.

2. Number to check
   - Hook: The number first-time trailer buyers miss is {{number}}.
   - Body: Show where to find it and why it matters.
   - CTA: Comment CHECKLIST.

3. Before you sign
   - Hook: Before you sign for a camper, ask this.
   - Body: One dealer question plus why it works.
   - CTA: Comment CHECKLIST.

4. Floorplan reality
   - Hook: This floorplan looks great until {{real_life_scenario}}.
   - Body: Explain the practical test.
   - CTA: Comment CHECKLIST.

5. Budget surprise
   - Hook: Your camper payment is not your camper budget.
   - Body: List 4 to 6 hidden first-year costs.
   - CTA: Comment CHECKLIST.

6. Delivery day warning
   - Hook: Do not leave the dealership until you test {{system}}.
   - Body: Explain the check.
   - CTA: Comment CHECKLIST.

7. Used trailer red flag
   - Hook: Used trailer deal? Look for this first.
   - Body: Water, roof, tires, soft floors, records, or appliances.
   - CTA: Comment CHECKLIST.

8. Starter gear reality
   - Hook: Buying the camper is not the end of the shopping list.
   - Body: Useful basics without overbuying.
   - CTA: Comment CHECKLIST.

9. Scorecard
   - Hook: If this score is low, slow down before buying.
   - Body: Tow fit, budget, floorplan, dealer confidence, delivery readiness.
   - CTA: Comment CHECKLIST.

10. Comment reply
   - Hook: Someone asked whether {{question}}.
   - Body: Answer one buyer question clearly.
   - CTA: Comment CHECKLIST.
